Authorization and gateway

A write needs a real signature over the real body from the right key; replays, other namespaces and reserved paths are refused.

93 tests in 15 files. Source: Typescript/tests/

namespaceWriteAuthorization.test.ts4 tests

A write is authorized only by a real signature over the real body from the right key; an echoed identityHash or a signature over another body is refused.

Signed writes are bound to the chain head: resending a body, an old grant or a write meant for another namespace fails.
